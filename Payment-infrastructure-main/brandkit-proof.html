09 Do / don't

Do
  • “We first saw this account 3 days ago”
  • Console: ALLOWED
  • “Recovers 94% of generated mule fan-out”
  • Three latency figures, total emphasised
  • ₹4,25,000.00 — en-IN, from paise
  • Hairlines, no shadows

Don't
  • “This account was created 3 days ago”
  • A green “Verified” badge on an allow
  • “94% of fraud detected”
  • A lone latency number, anywhere
  • ₹425,000.00 — 3-3-3 grouping
  • Gradients, blur, dark mode, spinners
  • Ultramarine as a pale tint behind text
